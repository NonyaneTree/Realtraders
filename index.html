Select Free GER30 Signals from the Technical Analysis dropdown to visit ger30.html.
Can beginners learn forex trading here?
Yes. The Forex Mentor section provides an entry point for learning trading terminology, strategies, market concepts and risk management.
What is fundamental forex analysis?
Fundamental analysis examines economic data, interest rates, inflation, employment reports and central bank decisions to understand factors that may affect market prices. Visit the Fundamental Analysis page to learn more.
Are trading signals guaranteed to make money?
No. No trading signal, strategy or analysis can guarantee profits. Markets can move unexpectedly, and traders can lose some or all of the money they risk.
