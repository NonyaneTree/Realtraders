GER30 Trading Strategy

No trading strategy wins every time. These concepts are educational tools for analysing the market and managing risk.

01 — Market Structure

Study the direction of the market and identify higher highs, higher lows, lower highs and lower lows. Use the broader trend as context for potential trading setups.

02 — Support and Resistance

Mark relevant price levels and observe how price behaves around them. A level is not a guarantee that the market will reverse or continue in a particular direction.

03 — Breakouts and Price Action

Study breakouts, retests, candle behaviour and momentum. Consider the possibility of false breakouts before deciding whether a setup fits your plan.

04 — Economic News

German and European economic releases, European Central Bank decisions and global market sentiment can influence the DAX. Be aware of scheduled news and changing volatility.

05 — Risk Management

Plan your position size and acceptable loss before entering a trade. Use risk controls appropriate to your account and remember that leveraged products can magnify losses.

What is the difference between GER30 and GER40?

GER30 is a legacy trading symbol associated with the DAX when the index contained 30 companies. The index expanded to 40 constituents in 2021. The symbol and product specifications depend on the broker.
